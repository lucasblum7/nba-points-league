Bonus point ideas

  • Underdog wins (tracked above): +1 / +2 / +3 for wins at +350 / +500 / +800 or longer.
  • Beat the preseason line: +3 for any team that finishes above its over/under.
  • Make the play-in: +2 for finishing 7th–10th in its conference.
  • Weekly player picks: before each Monday–Sunday week, every manager picks one NBA player for each of points, rebounds, and assists. The pick with the league’s highest weekly average in a category gets bonus points (for example +2 each).

League ideas

  • Beat Vegas prize: a side prize for whoever’s teams beat their preseason lines by the most combined.
  • Next year’s draft order: reverse order of this year’s finish, or a lottery weighted toward the bottom.
  • Tiebreaker: head-to-head record, then deepest playoff run, then most weeks won.
  • Weekly head-to-head: three matchups each week, with a separate win–loss record.
  • Small stakes: perks for each weekly winner and a punishment for last place.
  • End-of-season awards: best value pick, biggest bust, best week, luckiest playoff run.
League rules

The Win Column

  • 1 point per regular-season win.
  • NBA Cup games (group play and knockouts): 2 points per win. They’re marked in gold on the scoreboard.
  • Make the playoffs: +5 (play-in winners count; play-in losers don’t).
  • 2 points per playoff game won (play-in games don’t count). NBA Cup winner: +5.
  • Ties: most playoff series won, then most regular-season wins.

Matchups

  • Three head-to-head matchups each Monday–Sunday week; everyone plays everyone once every 5 weeks.
  • Best net record that week (wins minus losses of your 5 teams) wins.
  • NBA Cup games count double: +2 for a win, −2 for a loss toward your net that week.
  • Ties: most total wins that week breaks it; otherwise the matchup is a tie.
  • Standings order: matchup record, then season net, then total wins.

Weekly winners

  • Most regular-season wins in each Monday–Sunday week.
  • Ties: shared. “Weeks won” counts finished weeks only.

Last night’s results

  • Winners keep their team colors; the owner who picked up the win gets +1.
